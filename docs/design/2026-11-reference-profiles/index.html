Reference profiles: thirty researcher homepages

Captured 6 November 2026 as the reference for round three. Click a capture for the full page. The captures stay local; the notes are in README.md.

Kevin Feng · PhD, UW; Microsoft

kjfeng.me

Two columns: sticky identity with a black-and-white photo; prose bio with linked names; featured publications with a small thumbnail each, venue in blue, awards in orange; dated updates with "show more"; a last-updated line. The personality is his own typeface.
