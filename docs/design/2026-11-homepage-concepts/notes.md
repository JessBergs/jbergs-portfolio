# Design notes, round two (concepts)

Round one shuffled the same hero, bio and image tiles into five layouts and was turned down.
The noise on the current page is the tiles themselves: twenty event banners, each in its own
typeface and colour. This round changes what an entry on the page is.

## What stays

Charter, the pastel frame around the viewport, and its five colours. The colours now mean
something: one per kind of work, used the same way on every concept.

| kind | wash | pen |
|---|---|---|
| speaking | #ffd6c4 | #b4521e |
| research | #c5dafd | #2f5fb3 |
| mentoring | #e6ccf3 | #6a44a8 |
| organising | #ffc8dd | #b8336a |
| judging | #ffdfa3 | #8a6414 |

Paper #fefcf9, ink #1d2029. Quicksand is dropped; Charter does the work, with one sans
where a concept needs one (Bricolage Grotesque for stamp lettering, Instrument Sans for
chart labels).

## One idea each

- A, Deck: entries are playing cards. Upcoming is the hand you are holding. Roles are tokens.
  Past work is the cards played, with a small round crest instead of a banner.
- B, Register: one ruled register read as time runs, with a "today" line. Open boxes above it,
  ticked below. No images in the flow; a thumbnail floats in on hover.
- C, Margin notes: no hero. One short piece of writing; dates, figures and roles sit in the
  margin beside the sentence that mentions them.
- D, Stamps: a passport. Circles for talks, rectangles for the rest, wide visas for standing
  roles. Upcoming stamps are outlined, not yet inked. Pages are years.
- E, Timeline: the hero is a chart. Five lanes, 2011 to next spring, today as a line, upcoming
  drawn hollow. The last three years get most of the width.

## Checked against the usual defaults

- No cream-plus-terracotta single accent: five inks, all from the frame.
- B is a ruled table because the content is a register; row washes, the check box state and
  the gradient today line keep it from reading as a broadsheet.
- A uses cards because they are literally cards: fixed 5:7, corner indices, inner frame.
- D uses uppercase because stamps do.
- No eyebrow labels, no middle-dot meta strings, no arrows on links, no mono data labels.
